**Figure caption:** Fig. 1 . Diagram of experimental design, including high-nutrient, low-chlorophyll (HNLC) regions of interest (subarctic North Pacific [SNP], equatorial Pacific [EQ], and Southern Ocean [SO]); NASA satellite products (sea surface temperature [SST], net primary production [NPP; from the CAFE algorithm], particulate carbon [PC; sum of particulate inorganic carbon, PIC, and particulate organic carbon, POC], instantaneous photosynthetically available radiation [ iPAR ], chlorophyll [ chl ], and normalized fluorescence line height [ nFLH ]); and climate modes (Pacific Decadal Oscillation [PDO], North Pacific Gyre Oscillation [NPGO], Southern Oscillation Index [SOI], bimonthly Multivariate El Niño–Southern Oscillation [ENSO] Index Version 2 [MEIv2], and Southern Annular Mode [SAM]). Nonphotochemical quenching (NPQ)-corrected ϕsat is computed from chl , iPAR , and nFLH as a proxy for Fe stress. The monthly averages of NPQ-corrected ϕ sat for January 2003 to December 2022 are averaged across HNLC regions for seasonal and interannual analysis. The climate modes and satellite products are correlated based on Pearson’s correlation at an α < 0.05 significance level for the interannual trends. EOF, empirical orthogonal function; SLP, sea level pressure; SSH, sea surface height.
